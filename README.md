## Featured Project

### Novel Artificial Neurons
*MSc Dissertation Project*

**Repository:** [github.com/berkedenizbozyigit/novel-artificial-neurons](https://github.com/berkedenizbozyigit/novel-artificial-neurons)  
**Preprint:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19344)

My MSc dissertation explores one of the most basic assumptions in neural networks: that a neuron should aggregate its inputs through a weighted sum.

This project investigates alternative aggregation mechanisms and looks at how they affect learning dynamics, robustness, and representational capacity. As part of the work, I implemented **F-Mean**, **Gaussian**, and **Hybrid** neuron variants in **PyTorch** and evaluated them on vision benchmarks.

**Selected Results**
- **87.61%** accuracy on **CIFAR-10** vs. **87.33%** baseline
- **0.991** robustness score vs. **0.890** for standard neurons
- Full implementation and experimentation in **PyTorch**


🥇 **Departmental Prize Winner** — awarded for the **best project** in the **2024/25 Artificial Intelligence MSc cohort** at the **[University of York](https://www.york.ac.uk/)**.
